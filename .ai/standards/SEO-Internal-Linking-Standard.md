# LFADJ SEO Internal Linking Standard

## 1. Purpose

The sole responsibility of this Standard is to establish a unified SEO internal linking framework that helps Google crawl the website more efficiently, understand its structure, build topical authority, improve page indexation and keyword rankings, and increase authentic inquiries.

## 2. SEO Objectives

- **Improve Crawling**：提高搜索引擎发现和访问网站内容的效率。
- **Improve Indexing**：提高重要页面被搜索引擎收录的能力。
- **Build Topical Authority**：增强网站在相关主题领域的整体权威性。
- **Distribute Page Authority**：在相关内容之间合理传递页面权威度。
- **Help Users Continue Reading**：帮助用户自然地继续阅读相关内容。
- **Increase Qualified Inquiries**：增加来自真实项目需求的高质量询盘。

## 3. Core Principles

- **User First**：内链必须首先帮助真实用户理解内容关系并继续获取有价值的信息，不得损害阅读清晰度或用户信任。
- **SEO First**：内链必须支持搜索引擎发现、理解和评估网站内容，不得削弱页面的搜索意图或网站的主题结构。
- **Relevance First**：内链必须建立在内容之间真实且直接的主题关联上，不得连接缺乏实质相关性的内容。
- **Natural Linking**：内链必须自然融入内容语境，不得为了增加链接数量而生硬插入或干扰内容表达。
- **One Link One Purpose**：每个内链必须承担一个明确且必要的目的，不得同时承载相互冲突或含糊不清的意图。

## 4. Mandatory Internal Linking Rules

The following relationships are mandatory because they establish the website hierarchy, connect topic clusters, support crawling and indexing, distribute authority to relevant pages, strengthen keyword relevance, and guide qualified users toward genuine inquiries.

### 4.1 Home

- **Must link to:** Products, Applications, Solutions, Manufacturing, Resources, and Contact.
- **SEO purpose:** Establish the primary site hierarchy, pass authority from the root page to every core topic area, and provide search engines with direct discovery paths to the website's principal content and conversion destination.

### 4.2 Products

- **Must link to:** Product Detail pages, relevant Applications, relevant Solutions, Manufacturing, relevant Resources, and Contact.
- **SEO purpose:** Connect the product taxonomy to individual product entities and their supporting use-case, solution, capability, knowledge, and inquiry content so search engines can understand the commercial topic cluster and index its deeper pages.

### 4.3 Product Detail

- **Must link to:** Products, relevant Applications, relevant Solutions, Manufacturing, relevant Resources, and Contact.
- **SEO purpose:** Preserve the parent-child product hierarchy, associate each product with its legitimate use cases and supporting expertise, prevent isolated detail pages, and create a clear path from product discovery to qualified inquiry.

### 4.4 Applications

- **Must link to:** relevant Products, relevant Product Detail pages, relevant Solutions, relevant Resources, and Contact.
- **SEO purpose:** Connect industry and project intent with suitable product entities and solution knowledge, reinforce application topic clusters, and help search engines distinguish informational context from commercial destinations.

### 4.5 Solutions

- **Must link to:** relevant Products, relevant Product Detail pages, relevant Applications, relevant Resources, Manufacturing when it substantiates the solution, and Contact.
- **SEO purpose:** Connect problem-solving intent with applicable products, use cases, supporting knowledge, and verified delivery capability while consolidating solution authority and supporting inquiry conversion.

### 4.6 Manufacturing

- **Must link to:** relevant Products, relevant Solutions, relevant Resources, and Contact.
- **SEO purpose:** Associate verified production and delivery capabilities with the commercial and solution topics they support, strengthen entity credibility, and prevent capability content from becoming an isolated topic.

### 4.7 Resources

- **Must link to:** relevant Products, relevant Product Detail pages, relevant Applications, relevant Solutions, relevant Manufacturing content, and Contact when the resource supports project evaluation.
- **SEO purpose:** Connect informational content to its authoritative commercial and technical context, extend topic coverage, pass authority from knowledge content to relevant core pages, and support progression from research to qualified inquiry.

### 4.8 Contact

- **Must link to:** Products, Applications, and Solutions.
- **SEO purpose:** Keep the inquiry destination connected to the website's main commercial and intent pathways, preserve crawl continuity, and help users return to relevant decision-making content before submitting a qualified inquiry.
